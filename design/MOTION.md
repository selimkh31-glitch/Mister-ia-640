# Mister IA 64 — Motion

## Personality

Quick, restrained, tactile. Motion should suggest competence and responsiveness, not spectacle.

## Rules

- Motion explains hierarchy, state or causality.
- Entrance effects must not delay comprehension or SEO content.
- Hover movement stays subtle; avoid large layout shifts.
- Prefer transform/opacity for visual transitions.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Do not animate every section simply because it enters the viewport.

## Timing targets

- Micro interaction: about 120–160ms.
- Standard transition: about 200–240ms.
- Editorial/hero movement: up to ~420ms only when justified.

## Approved character

- small upward/opacity entrance for selected high-signal elements;
- precise button/arrow feedback;
- modest card lift where cards are actually interactive;
- smooth but short navigation state changes.

## Forbidden by default

- perpetual glows;
- particle fields;
- floating AI orbs;
- parallax that harms reading;
- springy/bouncy motion on serious conversion actions;
- staggered animation on every paragraph or SEO block.
