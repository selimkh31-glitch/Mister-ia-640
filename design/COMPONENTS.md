# Mister IA 64 — Component Rules

## Principle

Reuse behavior and engineering; adapt composition and expression to Mister IA 64.

## Buttons

- Primary: deep green surface, high-contrast text, restrained radius.
- Accent: citron only when the action deserves elevated emphasis.
- Secondary/text actions: simple and editorial; avoid unnecessary button chrome.
- Do not add new heights, radii or shadows casually.

## Cards

Cards are for meaningful grouping. A page should not become a wall of equal cards.

Approved families can include:

- service/problem cards;
- client case studies;
- audit/offer summary;
- process steps.

Each family should have a distinct information role.

## Navigation

- Prioritise services, proof and contact.
- Keep mobile navigation simple and fast.
- Sticky behavior must not dominate small screens.

## Forms

- Labels stay visible.
- Focus state is obvious.
- Errors are textual, not color-only.
- Inputs remain practical on mobile.

## SEO / local pages

Local landing pages may share content primitives and data, but should not all render as cloned homepages. Vary composition according to intent while keeping the same brand system.

## New components

Before creating one, check whether an existing primitive can be adapted. If a new visual pattern becomes recurring, document it here before spreading it across pages.
