# Mister IA 64 — Design Bible

## Visual archetype
Modern local digital studio with editorial confidence. Human and useful before technological.

## Current visual DNA to preserve
- Deep green identity centered on `#163b35`.
- Acid-citron accent around `#e4f57b`, used selectively.
- Warm off-white canvas around `#fbfcf8`.
- Muted green surfaces instead of cold grey UI.
- Sans-serif body language with a restrained serif/italic editorial accent.
- Strong whitespace and clear section rhythm.
- Slightly handcrafted touches are welcome when controlled.

## Composition
- Density: medium-low on marketing pages.
- Use large editorial compositions for high-value messages.
- Avoid converting every idea into a card.
- Cards must group real information, not exist as decoration.
- Alternate section structures so long SEO pages do not become repetitive template stacks.
- Keep strong local/business proof visible early.
- Prefer numbered editorial rows and continuous sections when they communicate better than cards.

## Typography
- Primary typography must stay highly readable and direct.
- Serif/italic treatment is an accent, not the default body style.
- H1 must carry one clear promise; decorative line breaks must not damage mobile reading.
- Avoid more than three simultaneous typographic voices.

## Color
Roles:
- Canvas: warm off-white.
- Primary text/action: deep green.
- Secondary surfaces: pale botanical greens.
- Accent: acid citron for emphasis, proof or selected calls to action.
- Error/destructive: separate from the brand accent.

Do not introduce purple/blue AI gradients, neon cyan, glass effects or unrelated brand colors without explicit design approval.

## Shape
- Radius philosophy: restrained, functional softness.
- Buttons and cards should not drift into pill-heavy SaaS language.
- Shadows remain subtle; hierarchy should primarily come from composition, contrast and spacing.

## Photography / imagery
Priority order:
1. Real local business/client photography.
2. Real screenshots or documented client work.
3. Purpose-built illustration tied to the service story.
4. Generated visuals only when they look intentional and project-specific.

Avoid generic robots, glowing brains, circuit heads, floating AI orbs and stock-office clichés.

## Icons
Lucide-style functional icons are acceptable. Do not use icons as filler when typography or imagery communicates better.

## Signature devices
1. Deep green + acid citron contrast.
2. Editorial serif emphasis inside otherwise direct modern typography.
3. Human-business framing: concrete work moments, local context and practical outcomes.
4. Editorial rows and proof strips instead of generic card walls.

## Homepage pilot
The homepage is the first implementation of this system. It uses a large local-first hero, a work-method panel rather than a fake dashboard, numbered friction rows, a continuous service stack, editorial client case rows, a human accompaniment split, and restrained offer sections.

## Anti-patterns
- generic centered SaaS hero with gradient headline;
- glassmorphism;
- repeated 3-card grids across the entire page;
- excessive badges/pills;
- meaningless dashboard mockups;
- decorative AI sparkle iconography;
- identical visual treatment across local SEO landing pages;
- importing another client's visual identity because the component already exists.
