# Mister IA 64 — Reference Policy

References are used for principles, not pixel copying.

For each future reference, record:

- source or URL;
- the principle being borrowed;
- what must not be copied;
- whether it affects typography, composition, imagery, motion or interaction.

The existing Mister IA 64 interface is the current internal baseline: botanical green/citron palette, warm off-white surfaces, editorial serif accents and human-business framing.

Prefer references that improve editorial clarity, local trust, case-study storytelling, legibility or disciplined interaction. Avoid adopting fashionable visual devices that do not solve a brand or usability problem.
