# Mister IA 64 — Brand

## Business

- Positioning: local AI, website and automation implementation partner for small businesses and independent professionals.
- Geography: Pays Basque first; remote delivery possible.
- Core promise: start from the client's real work, simplify what matters, and make digital tools understandable and useful.

## Audience

Local business owners, artisans and small teams who want more visibility and less operational friction without becoming technical experts.

## Brand attributes

- Human
- Local
- Capable
- Precise
- Contemporary
- Accessible

## Voice

Plain French, concrete business examples, no AI hype, no inflated claims, no startup jargon when simpler language exists.

## Visual identity must feel like

A modern local digital studio with an artisan mindset: calm confidence, editorial clarity, practical detail and a recognisable green/citron identity.

## It must not feel like

- anonymous SaaS;
- sci-fi AI company;
- neon cyber interface;
- enterprise consultancy;
- cheap template marketplace;
- another Mister IA client site.

## Trust hierarchy

1. Human presence and clear explanation.
2. Real client work and outcomes.
3. Local relevance.
4. Process and price clarity.
5. Technology only after the business problem is understood.
